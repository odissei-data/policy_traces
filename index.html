ODISSEI

Government Documents citing Social Science Data Infrastructure

Parliamentary papers, attachments and official publications of the Dutch government that cite research built on CBS microdata, the LISS panel or ODISSEI Grants. About this data

Searches document titles, organisations, dossier numbers and document ids, and the titles, authors and DOIs of cited publications, and the data source (CBS microdata, LISS panel, ODISSEI Grants). Citing text is not searched.
